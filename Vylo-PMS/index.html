The failure modes other portfolio tools shrug at

Each of these started as a real problem in a real portfolio — not a feature-list item invented for a demo.

RE

Real estate and gold, tracked like real assets

A 20-year home loan doesn't inflate your holdings the day you sign the paperwork. Direct Real Estate and Physical Gold get their own installment schedules — quantity pinned at one, EMI or savings plans generated automatically, outstanding loan balance tracked alongside.

SC

Nothing counts until it's actually paid

Generate a 20-year installment schedule and your Dashboard won't pretend all 240 payments already happened. Every installment sits as Scheduled until its date arrives and you confirm it — one at a time, or all overdue rows at once.

RA

Risk fields that match what you actually hold

A Fixed Deposit doesn't need a Beta or a Leverage field, so it doesn't get one. Every risk field shows or hides itself based on the instrument's real vehicle type — and the backend enforces it, not just the screen.

PL

A planning workflow that reconciles itself

Goals, risk appetite, requirements, targets, gap analysis, rebalance, investment plan — seven steps that read the three numbers most tools never ask for and show you the actual gap between them, not just a target percentage.

IM

Bulk import without importing someone else's mistake

Every imported row is checked against its portfolio's market and currency before it commits. One bad row gets flagged and skipped — the other two hundred still go through.

SS

A score weighted for what's actually applicable

Seven scoring dimensions, but not every one applies to every asset class — a Fixed Deposit's score isn't dragged down by a Beta it was never supposed to have. Missing data and inapplicable data are shown as two different things, not blurred into one bad number.

RP

Reports which generate the data you actually need

A PDF statement report that looks like something you'd actually send to a co-owner, a lender, or your own records — not a printed screenshot of a browser tab.

AD

An admin panel built for total control

Role-based access enforced at the API, not just the UI. A full audit log. Backup and restore with a mandatory safety snapshot before anything is overwritten, and a typed confirmation so a restore is never one misclick away.

Built to be trusted with the numbers, not just to show them

The controls a portfolio tool should have had from day one.

Tenant isolation

Every portfolio-scoped request is checked against a strict per-account reach rule — no shared visibility by default, ever.

Full audit log

Every sensitive action — user changes, role reassignment, restores — is recorded and filterable by the Owner.

Safety-net restore

Every restore takes a mandatory pre-restore snapshot first, with a typed confirmation before anything is overwritten.

Role-based access

Owner, Family Member, Financial Advisor, and Small-Firm roles — each with exactly the access that role should have, no more.

Offline account recovery

Forgot your password? Recovery works with one-time codes generated on your own machine — no email, no reset link, no password-reset event ever leaves your computer.

Bcrypt password hashing

Passwords and recovery codes are hashed with bcrypt before storage — never kept in plaintext, anywhere.

No telemetry, ever

No analytics, no crash reporting, no license check calling home. The only outbound traffic is the market-price lookups you configure yourself — and any AI features run against a model on your own machine, not a cloud service.
